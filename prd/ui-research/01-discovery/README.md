> Status: 🟡 In progress
> Goal: deeply understand the problem and the user *before* proposing any solution.
> Diverge — explore widely, don't converge yet.

## Findings

- **`current-state-journey.md`** — the end-to-end journey map: cadence/volume,
  6 lifecycle stages with pain points and emotion, the event-readiness audit,
  the headline "confidence before it matters" insight, scope picture, pain→feature
  map, and JTBD list.
- **`competitor-teardown.md`** — landscape of DJ library/set tools. Key finding:
  **no commercial tool does acquisition** — Spotify→local-mp3 is our unoccupied wedge.
  Lexicon patterns to steal (scans/health, smartlists, Track Timeline, analytics fields,
  incoming-tracks dedup, Spotify export), and a scope-discipline list of what NOT to copy.
- **`lexicon-personal-impressions.md`** — Sagiv's first-hand reactions: likes (tab-per-category
  IA, Sync separate from Tracks, main Tracks page, inline preview, VDJ-as-data-source),
  dislikes (no album art, dated visuals, useless Key field), and the design implications
  (Spotify-like visual direction; album art required; VDJ-integration feasibility spike).

## Exit criteria (when Discovery is "done")

- A clear picture of the user's goals and pains (✅ from journaling).
- Comparative analysis of how existing tools solve adjacent problems (✅ from teardown).
- Enough understanding to write a sharp problem statement in Define without guessing (✅).

**Discovery is effectively complete.** Optional remaining: lightweight personas, and a
deeper Djoid/DJ.Studio teardown *if/when* set-building is phased into scope (Define decides).
