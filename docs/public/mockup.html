Sidelist

Your big library is hard to scan when picking songs. The sidelist is a small, curated shortlist you build sets from — recently-added songs plus the favorites you know you want to play soon. Add a song with the ★ on any row, work mostly from this list, and dip into the full library only when you need to. Top it up over time, then clear it and start fresh.

Tagging

openbeat will help you tag your music — energy (high / mid / low) and characteristics (funky, latin, sexy, dark, emotional…) — faster and with fewer missed tags, so when you DJ you can quickly find the right song for any moment in the set.
